APRS-IS feed

Feed servers

Coverage feed

Filtered APRS-IS port for your registered stations. Leave blank for the defaults from .env. If your igates connect to your own aprsc, point this at it too (its normal filtered port, usually 14580), so both feeds see the same packets.

Duplicates feed

APRS-IS passes on only the first copy of a packet. When several igates gate the same packet, aprsc drops the later copies, and its duplicates feed (a dupefeed listener in aprsc.conf) sends those copies out. With your igates connected to your own aprsc, this shows every igate and digi that heard a packet, not only the first one. Copies of one packet count once in packet totals, and once for each station that heard it. Read-only (passcode -1), no filter. Use a login different from the coverage feed's.

Rejected packets (data quality)

Packets from registered mobiles that were dropped: not over RF, ambiguous or bad positions, impossible jumps, duplicates. Shows the newest 10; rejects are kept for 2 days (all of them are on the Logs tab).

Remote config

Commands are sent from this callsign, so use one you are licensed for. The passcode is calculated from it. Use an SSID that nothing else is logged in to APRS-IS with (not your igate's), or APRS-IS drops that station's connection while you send. Remembered in this browser only, never on the server.

Reads and changes settings on LoRa FieldOps igates and digis with APRS messages (CSR read, CSU unlock, CSW write), sent over a separate APRS-IS connection as that callsign; the coverage feed stays read-only. The connection closes after 5 minutes without a command. An igate gets them over its own APRS-IS connection. A digi gets them only if an igate with IS-to-RF downlink has heard it directly in the last 30 minutes. The node must have remote config turned on (serial remotecfg on), and writes need its unlock token. Each command can take up to 60 s, and only one runs at a time.
