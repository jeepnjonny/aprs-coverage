Run two devices for weeks or months and see which does better on average. Day-to-day results swing with traffic, weather and where people drove, so look at the trend and the confidence bands, not any single day.

Counts only packets sent while the two were within m of each other, e.g. both trackers in one car. The fairest test.
Counts only squares driven in both periods, so a detour or a new road doesn't look like a change.
